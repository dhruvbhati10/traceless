## 3. What the App Does

Traceless removes metadata from copies of your photos and videos. This includes EXIF data, GPS location, IPTC creator fields, camera maker notes, XMP records, and C2PA provenance manifests, according to the cleaning profile you select.

**Your original files are never modified.** Traceless writes cleaned copies and leaves your originals untouched. You are responsible for choosing which copy you keep, share, or delete.

## 4. Important Limitations

Please read this section carefully.

* **No guarantee of anonymity.** Removing metadata reduces the information attached to a file. It does not make a file anonymous. The visible content of a photo or video can still identify a person, a place, or a device.
* **No defence against image forensics.** Traceless does not alter the pixels of your media to defeat forensic analysis, sensor-noise fingerprinting, or reverse image search. It is a metadata tool, not an anonymisation tool.
* **Cleaning profiles matter.** If you select a profile other than Maximum, you are deliberately choosing to keep some categories of metadata. In that mode the App also skips the full re-encode pass, so a provenance manifest may survive. Use Maximum when removal must be certain.
* **Verify before you rely on it.** The App shows you exactly what it removed and what remains. If the consequences of a leak matter to you, check the "Remaining" view before sharing a file.
* **Third-party services may add data back.** Once you upload a file elsewhere, that service may attach its own metadata. That is outside our control.
