## Common questions

**Does Traceless change my original photos?**
No. Traceless only ever writes cleaned copies. Your originals are left exactly as they were.

**Does anything get uploaded?**
No. The app has no networking code at all. Everything happens on your device.

**Why did a file get re-encoded?**
Apple's imaging frameworks cannot strip a C2PA provenance manifest from a container. When one survives the fast pass, Traceless decodes and re-encodes the image from raw pixels, which is the only way to guarantee it is gone. The detail screen tells you which method was used.

**Why is some metadata still there?**
Check your cleaning profile. Anything other than Maximum deliberately keeps some categories. Open the "Remaining" tab on a file to see exactly what is left.
